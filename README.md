### About

OneWeather keeps the Samsung weather look and makes it more useful.
The background follows the weather and the time of day, and soft forecast cards sit on top without hiding it.

### Features

**Adaptive look**
- Background changes with the current weather and the time of day
- Soft forecast cards over the background, so the scene stays visible

**Forecast**
- Hourly forecast for the next 48 hours in a clear, structured view
- Day by day forecast for the week
- Visibility, pressure, wind speed, sunrise and sunset times, UV index, humidity and more
- Most weather elements expand in place, without sending you to weather.com

**Widgets**
- A choice of home screen widgets
- Widget colors adapt to the weather and the time of day
- Pick a shiny border or a One UI style blurred look
